5.55 minutes apart. The pooled average sits between the two and describes neither: it reports a formula that is roughly +7.79 minutes optimistic, and hides that it is 2.2 times as wrong at one store as at the other.

Synthetic data. The events come from a seeded generator and defects are injected on purpose. The gap measures the store speeds written into that generator, not a real fleet. What this shows is that the pipeline finds a per-store difference that a single pooled figure would report as one problem.
